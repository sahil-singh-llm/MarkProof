# Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose

MarkProof is a local desktop tool for organizing trademark proof-of-use evidence bundles. It helps a user structure evidence by case, goods and services, time period, territory, metadata, and export order.

## Primary Users

- Legal operations staff preparing evidence for attorney review.
- Trademark paralegals organizing proof-of-use documents.
- Hiring managers reviewing this project as a TypeScript and Electron portfolio piece.

## Brand And Tone

The interface should feel careful, restrained, and evidence-oriented. It should avoid legal certainty language and avoid decorative visual noise. The product should look trustworthy without pretending to be a legal authority.

## Strategic Principles

- Local-first and transparent.
- Deterministic instead of AI-generated.
- Secure Electron boundaries.
- Dense enough for repeated professional use.
- Clear auditability without overstating forensic guarantees.
