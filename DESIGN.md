# Design Notes

## Register

Product UI. The design serves a task-focused desktop tool.

## Physical Scene

A paralegal or legal operations user reviews confidential evidence on a work laptop in a normal office setting, comparing dates, goods, services, and document metadata before attorney review.

## Color Strategy

Restrained. Use lightly tinted neutrals, one teal primary accent for active states and primary actions, and semantic colors only for status.

## Typography

Use the system UI font stack. Keep hierarchy compact and readable. Do not use display typography for labels, tables, or controls.

## Layout

Prefer a familiar desktop app shell with sidebar navigation, a top workspace bar, and dense content regions. Use cards only for distinct repeated records or focused panels.

## Copy

Use precise product language: coverage detected, gap detected, review recommended. Do not use "legally sufficient" or similar legal conclusion wording.
